# National Standards for Blood Transfusion Services
**Issuing Organization:** National Blood Transfusion Council (NBTC) & DGHS, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Publication Date:** 2022-04-15 | **Effective Date:** 2022-05-01  
**Category:** Blood Bank Operations & Donor Criteria

## SEC-4.2: Donor Selection Criteria and Deferral Intervals (Page 28)
For whole blood donation, the minimum inter-donation interval must be 90 days (12 weeks) for male donors and 120 days (16 weeks) for female donors. Donors must weigh at least 45 kg for a 350 mL donation and at least 55 kg for a 450 mL donation. Pre-donation hemoglobin must be at least 12.5 g/dL (copper sulphate method or automated hematology analyzer). Donors with active infection, acute fever, jaundice history within 12 months, or major surgery within 6 months must be deferred.

*Keywords: donation interval, deferral, hemoglobin requirement, minimum weight, female donation interval, male donation interval*

## SEC-5.1: ABO and RhD Grouping Requirements (Page 42)
Every blood unit and patient sample must undergo forward (cell) grouping using anti-A, anti-B, and anti-D monoclonal sera, and reverse (serum) grouping using known A, B, and O pooled reagent red blood cells. Discrepancies between forward and reverse grouping must be resolved before releasing blood components. Weak D (Du) testing must be performed on all blood donor samples that are non-reactive on immediate spin with anti-D.

*Keywords: ABO grouping, forward grouping, reverse grouping, RhD, weak D, grouping discrepancy*

## SEC-5.7: Emergency Uncrossmatched Blood Release Protocol (Page 56)
In life-threatening hemorrhagic shock where delays would result in patient fatality, uncrossmatched blood may be issued only upon receipt of a signed written emergency request by the treating clinician. O-Negative uncrossmatched packed red blood cells must be issued for females of childbearing potential (under 50 years). O-Positive RBCs may be issued for adult males and post-menopausal females if O-Negative supplies are critically depleted. Immediate standard crossmatch must be initiated retrospectively, and the clinician must be notified immediately if incompatibility is detected.

*Keywords: emergency release, uncrossmatched blood, O-negative emergency, massive transfusion, retrospective crossmatch*

## SEC-6.3: Acute Transfusion Reactions and Hemovigilance Protocol (Page 71)
In the event of an acute adverse transfusion reaction (fever > 1°C increase, chills, rigors, dyspnea, hypotension, dark urine, or flank pain), the transfusion must be stopped immediately. Intravenous access must be maintained with normal saline. The blood unit, infusion set, post-transfusion patient blood sample, and urine sample must be returned immediately to the blood bank for clerical check, direct antiglobulin test (DAT), repeat ABO/Rh typing, and visual hemolysis check. The incident must be documented and reported to the National Hemovigilance Programme of India (HvPI).

*Keywords: transfusion reaction, acute hemolytic reaction, febrile reaction, stop transfusion, DAT, hemovigilance, HvPI*
